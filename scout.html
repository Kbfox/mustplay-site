How it works

The page sends your question plus a snapshot of the list above to a Cloudflare Worker. The Worker runs an LLM tool loop over Qloo's /search, /v2/insights and /v2/tags endpoints, attaches IGDB covers, and streams each step back as server-sent events. Nothing is stored server-side; your list lives only in this browser.
